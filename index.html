The twelve steps

Ours differ slightly from the originals.

  1. We admitted we were powerless over the chart.
  2. We came to believe a wallet greater than ourselves could restore us.
  3. We wrote the seed phrase on paper and immediately lost the paper.
  4. We made a searching inventory of every bag we still hold.
  5. We admitted to another human being the exact size of the loss.
  6. We were entirely ready to stop checking the price at 4am.
  7. We humbly asked to be removed from every telegram group.
  8. We made a list of all the tickers we had harmed.
  9. We made direct amends, except when doing so would require selling.
  10. We continued to take inventory, and when we were wrong, we averaged down.
  11. We sought through meditation to improve our contact with support levels.
  12. Having had a spiritual awakening, we decided to do just one more trade.
